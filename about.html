About Aligned Learning Resources

Aligned Learning Resources creates clear, curriculum-aligned educational practice for Canadian classrooms. Our goal is to give students focused opportunities to practise important skills while giving teachers easy-to-use learning resources.

This website provides online practice organized by grade, curriculum, and topic. Printable classroom resources are also available through our Teachers Pay Teachers store.

For Teachers

Our printable resources are designed to complement classroom instruction and independent practice. Printable classroom resources are available in our Aligned Learning Resources TPT Store.
